Across the region

  • Catastrophic health spending affects from 1.2% of households in Ireland to 19.5% in Georgia. The median across 42 countries is 5.8%.
  • Out-of-pocket payments are more than 15% of current spending on health in 36 of 53 countries.
  • Unmet need for health care ranges up to 12.1% of people (Greece, 2024). The median across 36 countries is 2.5%.
