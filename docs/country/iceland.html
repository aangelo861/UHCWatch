Who goes without care?

In 2020, 2.8% of people had unmet need for health care: 6.9% in the poorest fifth and 1.2% in the richest. In 2020, 8.3% of people had unmet need for dental care: 12.4% in the poorest fifth and 3.3% in the richest. 4.5% of people needing care could not afford a prescribed medicine (2019).

How much do people pay out of pocket?

Out-of-pocket payments were 14.9% of current spending on health in 2024. The median across 53 countries is 22.0%.

How much does government spend on health?

Public spending on health was 7.5% of GDP in 2024. The median across 53 countries is 5.5%. Health took 16.1% of government spending (2023).
