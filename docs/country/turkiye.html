How many households face financial hardship?

In 2018, 4.3% of households had catastrophic health spending. In the poorest fifth of households it was 16.2%; in the richest fifth, 0.9%. Among households with catastrophic spending, dental care accounted for the largest share of out-of-pocket payments (41.3%). 2.6% of households were impoverished or further impoverished by out-of-pocket payments (2018).

Who goes without care?

In 2025, 0.7% of people had unmet need for health care: 2.1% in the poorest fifth and 0.2% in the richest. In 2025, 0.8% of people had unmet need for dental care: 1.8% in the poorest fifth and 0.2% in the richest. 6.2% of people needing care could not afford a prescribed medicine (2019).

How much do people pay out of pocket?

Out-of-pocket payments were 19.1% of current spending on health in 2023. The median across 53 countries is 22.0%. Households spent 2.4% of their budget on out-of-pocket payments for health care (2018).

How much does government spend on health?

Public spending on health was 3.3% of GDP in 2023. The median across 53 countries is 5.5%. Health took 9.8% of government spending (2023).
