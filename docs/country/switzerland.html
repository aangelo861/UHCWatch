How many households face financial hardship?

In 2017, 2.7% of households had catastrophic health spending. In the poorest fifth of households it was 11.3%; in the richest fifth, 0.4%. 1.5% of households were impoverished or further impoverished by out-of-pocket payments (2017).

Who goes without care?

In 2024, 1.4% of people had unmet need for health care: 2.3% in the poorest fifth and 0.7% in the richest. In 2024, 5.1% of people had unmet need for dental care: 8.4% in the poorest fifth and 1.4% in the richest.

How much do people pay out of pocket?

Out-of-pocket payments were 22.0% of current spending on health in 2023. The median across 53 countries is 22.0%.

How much does government spend on health?

Public spending on health was 7.9% of GDP in 2023. The median across 53 countries is 5.5%. Health took 11.7% of government spending (2023).

VHI role in filling gaps in coverage

VHI does not fill gaps in population coverage or covers user charges. VHI plays a complementary (services) role covering services that are excluded from mandatory health insurance and a supplementary role providing faster access to and greater choice of inpatient care. In 2023 VHI accounted for 7% of current spending on health (WHO, 2026) and covered around 78% of the population in 2021. Expanding VHI is likely to increase inequality in access to health care.
