How many households face financial hardship?

In 2015, 11.6% of households had catastrophic health spending. In the poorest fifth of households it was 44.2%; in the richest fifth, 0.7%. Among households with catastrophic spending, medicines accounted for the largest share of out-of-pocket payments (72.2%). 5.9% of households were impoverished or further impoverished by out-of-pocket payments (2015).

Who goes without care?

In 2024, 1.0% of people had unmet need for health care: 1.6% in the poorest fifth and 0.9% in the richest. In 2024, 0.6% of people had unmet need for dental care: 1.3% in the poorest fifth and 0.1% in the richest. 5.5% of people needing care could not afford a prescribed medicine (2019).

How much do people pay out of pocket?

Out-of-pocket payments were 23.1% of current spending on health in 2023. The median across 53 countries is 22.0%. Households spent 4.8% of their budget on out-of-pocket payments for health care (2015).

How much does government spend on health?

Public spending on health was 4.7% of GDP in 2023. The median across 53 countries is 5.5%. Health took 9.5% of government spending (2023).
