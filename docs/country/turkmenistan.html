How much do people pay out of pocket?

Out-of-pocket payments were 77.4% of current spending on health in 2023. The median across 53 countries is 22.0%.

How much does government spend on health?

Public spending on health was 0.9% of GDP in 2023. The median across 53 countries is 5.5%. Health took 8.6% of government spending (2023).
