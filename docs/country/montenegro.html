How many households face financial hardship?

In 2021, 8.7% of households had catastrophic health spending. In the poorest fifth of households it was 32.7%; in the richest fifth, 1.0%. Among households with catastrophic spending, medicines accounted for the largest share of out-of-pocket payments (76.4%). 4.0% of households were impoverished or further impoverished by out-of-pocket payments (2021).

Who goes without care?

In 2022, 2.6% of people had unmet need for health care: 3.8% in the poorest fifth and 1.4% in the richest. In 2022, 2.0% of people had unmet need for dental care: 4.2% in the poorest fifth and 0.1% in the richest.

How much do people pay out of pocket?

Out-of-pocket payments were 26.3% of current spending on health in 2023. The median across 53 countries is 22.0%. Households spent 4.8% of their budget on out-of-pocket payments for health care (2021).

How much does government spend on health?

Public spending on health was 6.7% of GDP in 2023. The median across 53 countries is 5.5%. Health took 16.6% of government spending (2023).
