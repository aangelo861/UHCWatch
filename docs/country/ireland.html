How many households face financial hardship?

In 2016, 1.2% of households had catastrophic health spending. In the poorest fifth of households it was 5.2%; in the richest fifth, 0.4%. Among households with catastrophic spending, inpatient care accounted for the largest share of out-of-pocket payments (72.5%). 0.8% of households were impoverished or further impoverished by out-of-pocket payments (2016).

Who goes without care?

In 2024, 2.9% of people had unmet need for health care: 4.0% in the poorest fifth and 2.5% in the richest. In 2024, 2.1% of people had unmet need for dental care: 3.6% in the poorest fifth and 1.0% in the richest. 4.3% of people needing care could not afford a prescribed medicine (2019).

How much do people pay out of pocket?

Out-of-pocket payments were 11.3% of current spending on health in 2023. The median across 53 countries is 22.0%. Households spent 2.2% of their budget on out-of-pocket payments for health care (2016).

How much does government spend on health?

Public spending on health was 5.3% of GDP in 2024. The median across 53 countries is 5.5%. Health took 22.2% of government spending (2023).
