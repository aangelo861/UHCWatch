Kazakhstan page

How many households face financial hardship?

In 2021, 5.8% of households had catastrophic health spending. In the poorest fifth of households it was 25.9%; in the richest fifth, 0.8%. Among households with catastrophic spending, medicines accounted for the largest share of out-of-pocket payments (40.6%). 3.6% of households were impoverished or further impoverished by out-of-pocket payments (2021).

How much do people pay out of pocket?

Out-of-pocket payments were 27.6% of current spending on health in 2023. The median across 53 countries is 22.0%. Households spent 3.3% of their budget on out-of-pocket payments for health care (2021).

How much does government spend on health?

Public spending on health was 2.5% of GDP in 2023. The median across 53 countries is 5.5%. Health took 10.7% of government spending (2023).
