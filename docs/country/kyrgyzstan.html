How much do people pay out of pocket?

Out-of-pocket payments were 42.3% of current spending on health in 2023. The median across 53 countries is 22.0%.

How much does government spend on health?

Public spending on health was 2.4% of GDP in 2023. The median across 53 countries is 5.5%. Health took 7.3% of government spending (2023).
