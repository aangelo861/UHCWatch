UHC Watch’s note on this indicator

Households with catastrophic health spending are households with out-of-pocket payments that are greater than 40% of their capacity to pay for health care, which may mean that they can no longer afford to meet other basic needs (food, housing and utilities).

Netherlands (Kingdom of the) cannot be compared to other countries because the Dutch household budget survey does not include the annual deductible amount households pay out of pocket for covered health care, biasing the results downwards.

Norway cannot be compared to other countries because the Norwegian household budget survey does not contain a retrospective component; consumption is recorded in a diary only, which may underestimate household spending on infrequent or irregular purchases, including out-of-pocket payments for infrequent or irregular types of health care.

Source: UHC Watch.
