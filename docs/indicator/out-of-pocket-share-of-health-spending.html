Out-of-pocket paymentsOut-of-pocket payments as a share of current spending on health

The part of a country’s current spending on health that people pay themselves at the point of use. The higher it is, the more the health system relies on payments by people who are ill.

Lower is better.

Why it matters

Catastrophic health spending is lower in countries where people pay a smaller share of health spending out of pocket.
