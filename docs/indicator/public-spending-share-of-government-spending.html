UHC Watch’s note on this indicator

Data are based on the System of Health Accounts (SHA) classification.

Public spending on health refers here to ‘transfers from government domestic revenue (allocated to health purposes)’ (SHA code FS 1) + ‘social insurance contributions’ (FS 3) only. It does not include compulsory pre-payment that is excluded from the denominator (government spending) – e.g. premiums for compulsory private health insurance covering user charges (co-payments) in France, premiums for compulsory substitutive private health insurance in Germany, flat-rate contributions for compulsory private health insurance in Netherlands (Kingdom of the) and employer and employee contributions for compulsory health insurance in Switzerland (FS 4).

Source: WHO Global Health Expenditure Database.
