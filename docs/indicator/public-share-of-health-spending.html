UHC Watch’s note on this indicator

Data are based on the System of Health Accounts (SHA) classification.

Public spending on health refers here to all compulsory health financing schemes (SHA code HF 1), including compulsory private health insurance (HF 1.2.2) in France, Germany, Netherlands (Kingdom of the) and Switzerland.
