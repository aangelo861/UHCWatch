UHC Watch’s note on this indicator

Data on the share of the population covered are for the same year as data on catastrophic health spending and may not reflect the current situation. Bosnia and Herzegovina reports different levels of population coverage for the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bosnia and Herzegovina (BIH-F) and Republika Srpska, Bosnia and Herzegovina (BIH-R). All references to Kosovo should be understood to be in the context of the United Nations Security Council resolution 1244 (1999).

Data on the share of the population covered are for 2017 in Latvia, 2018 in North Macedonia and Ukraine, 2019 in Cyprus and Serbia, 2022 in Malta, 2023 in Armenia and Tajikistan and 2024 in the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bosnia and Herzegovina (BIH-F), Republika Srpska, Bosnia and Herzegovina (BIH-R) and Kosovo*.

Source: data on population coverage from OECD Statistics for OECD countries and UHC Watch Policy explorer for other countries; data on catastrophic health spending from UHC Watch Indicator explorer; data on the main basis for entitlement from UHC Watch Policy explorer.
